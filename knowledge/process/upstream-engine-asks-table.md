The asks below are introduced upstream in issue [#43](https://github.com/M0Rf30/stremio-server-go/issues/43) (posted 2026-10-05), which serves as the tracking issue: small fixes and knobs first, then preparation, header cache, transcode-ahead and YouTube formats, then an issue per large item (hardware decode, the ABR ladder, remux, an event stream). A review of upstream `main` at `e127192` found nothing core must adapt to; see the notes in [[process/engine-upstream-prs]]. The maintainer closed #43 on 2026-10-09 ("feel free to re-open"); a re-check against `b85b668` (2026-10-13) narrowed item 3, added a constraint to item 6 and added item 14, and the checklist is posted on #43 (2026-10-13), asking the maintainer to re-open it, since only a maintainer can. The same review found three things on our side: the reference engine sets `STREMIO_HLS_SEGMENT_CACHE_BYTES=-1` (the new per-session cache cap, by default about 300 segments, would evict long and persisted sessions); core's engine module treats the new `503` + `Retry-After` from `/create` (`STREMIO_MAX_ENGINES`, default 20) as `engine-busy` ([[architecture/engine-prepare-stream]], built 2026-10-14); and the new `Host` allowlist (`STREMIO_ALLOWED_HOSTS`) doesn't affect core, which reaches the engine by IP literal.

**The maintainer's answers** (on #43, 2026-10-05): item 5 (`api_password` in the engine's access-log redaction list) is fixed on upstream `main` in `a038e80`; the small items (1–4) are fine as described (the private-host allowlist checked at dial time in both the relay and `/proxy`; for `BANDWIDTH`, keep the unconfigured legacy strings, no knob needed for the derived path); the medium items (6–9) are welcome, with preparation and transcode-ahead sharing one low-priority scheduler; hardware decode is accepted (off by default, per codec, software fallback); the ABR ladder is accepted as a new session shape behind a setting, design issue first; **remux stays on the fork**; the event stream comes later, after preparation and the ladder. Order: the small fixes, then preparation, then hardware decode, then the ladder (so the ladder builds on hardware frames). One area per PR; #43 is the tracker, plus one design issue per large item. Item numbers in the Status column below are #43's.

**Merged** (v0.18.0 and v0.18.1; details in [[process/engine-upstream-prs]]): `DELETE /hlsv2/{id}` and TTL `0` (#24), per-session overrides (#25), persistent sessions (#26), software HDR→SDR tone mapping (#27), the QSV preset (#28), not caching broken segments (#29), the container-aware seek pre-roll (#33, from issue #30), restoring persisted sessions by the reaper's TTL (#34), the Windows test fixes (#35) and a Windows CI job (#36, after v0.18.1). On upstream `main`, not yet in a release: since 2026-10-15, `BANDWIDTH` as the peak (#50), relayed playlists resolved against the post-redirect URL (#51), the short-segment check (#52) and `502`/`504` for a source that fails mid-transcode (#53). Since 2026-10-16, `STREMIO_HLS_PRIVATE_ALLOW` (#49) and the follow-ups to the maintainer's notes: #57 (#52), #58 (#53), #59 (#50), #60 (issue #48, now closed) and #61 (a test that failed on Windows). Open: #54 (DASH sniffing; reworked 2026-10-16 after a second review, with the compression handling split out as #66, stacked on it), and the follow-ups to the maintainer's post-merge notes: #62 (README rewrap, for #59), #63 (offline tests, for #60) and #64 (the second Windows-flaky relay test, for #61). Issue [#65](https://github.com/M0Rf30/stremio-server-go/issues/65) (2026-10-16) reports two things: a `mediaURL` on the engine's own origin skips the relay, so `/proxy/stream` can feed ffmpeg an unguarded remote playlist; and decimal bitrates break `BANDWIDTH` and session restore.
